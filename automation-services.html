    Industrial Automation Services for Smarter Manufacturing

    Hari Pallet Machinery (HPM) provides industrial automation services and engineering solutions designed to support efficient manufacturing operations. Our automation service areas cover PLC programming, SCADA and HMI development, industrial motor drives, machine communication, CAD design and manufacturing data reporting.

    From machine control and process visualisation to engineering documentation and performance monitoring, HPM helps businesses explore industrial automation solutions for their equipment and production requirements. Share your application, existing systems and operational goals with our team to discuss a suitable approach.

    INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION SOLUTIONS

    Automation, Control Systems and Engineering Services

    Effective factory automation brings together control hardware, operator interfaces, industrial communication and production data. HPM's automation service areas cover these connected requirements to help businesses improve machine visibility, streamline operations and make informed production decisions.

    PLC Programming & Industrial Automation

    Industrial PLC programming and automation solutions for machine control, equipment operation and production processes. Control requirements can be assessed around the application, connected equipment and operating sequence.

    Technologies & Platforms:

    Siemens TIA Portal · SIMATIC Manager · Mitsubishi GX Works3 · Allen-Bradley · Fatek PLC · Delta WPLSoft

    SCADA & HMI Development

    SCADA development and HMI design for operator interface screens, industrial process visualisation and monitoring. PLC-SCADA-HMI integration helps present machine and process information through an operator-focused interface.

    Technologies & Platforms:

    SCADA Development · Screen Design · SCADA Scripting · HMI Design · FactoryTalk View · PLC-SCADA/HMI Integration

    Industrial Drives & Communication

    Industrial VFD and motor-drive solutions, together with communication technologies that support connectivity between automation devices and control systems. The appropriate configuration depends on the machine and application requirements.

    Technologies & Platforms:

    Siemens VFD · Schneider VFD · Allen-Bradley VFD · Emerson · Modbus · RS-485 · Industrial Networking

    CAD Design, Data & Engineering Software

    Engineering drawings, technical documentation and data-management support for industrial machinery and manufacturing workflows. CAD tools and software can be used to prepare and organise relevant engineering information.

    Tools & Technologies:

    AutoCAD 2D/3D · Microsoft Excel · Microsoft Word · SQL · MS SQL · IIS
